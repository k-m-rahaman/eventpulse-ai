Planners typically coordinate 15 to 20 independent vendors and lose 15 to 20 hours per event to manual spreadsheet updates.When 50 unexpected guests arrive, heavy rain forces an outdoor ceremony indoors, or a caterer is delayed by 45 minutes, static spreadsheets break. EventPulse AI replaces brittle checklists with a live, ripple-effect crisis simulation engine.✨ Flagship Pillars & Capabilities<table>
